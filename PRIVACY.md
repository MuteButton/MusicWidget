# OnCue privacy policy

Developer: **MuteButton**. Effective date: **October 24, 2026**.

## Notification access and media information

Android notification access grants access to notifications. OnCue uses media-session information and media-notification destinations to display track titles, artists, artwork and progress, control playback, and reopen the playing app or browser tab. It does not save notification messages or a listening history. Live media information and destinations are held in app memory and released when sessions end or the listener disconnects.

## Local settings and Android backup

OnCue stores your chosen player, last player, glass-color preference, and optional browser URLs in private app storage. Android may back up or transfer these settings according to your device settings. The app backup rules include only its settings file; they exclude artwork, media-session information and diagnostic files. Notification access must be granted on each device. Device backup services are operated by your platform provider, not MuteButton.

## Other apps and data sharing

The player chooser queries launchable apps and browser capabilities on your device. OnCue has no Internet permission, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or developer-operated server. It does not transmit your media information to MuteButton. Opening a media app or saved URL hands control to the selected app; its own privacy policy applies.

## Retention and your choices

Settings remain until you change them, clear app storage or uninstall. Revoke notification access in Android Settings to stop media access. Clear a saved URL in setup to remove it. Manage existing device backups through your Android backup settings. OnCue does not create user accounts.

## Contact and policy updates

Contact MuteButton through the [project support page](https://github.com/MuteButton/OnCue/issues). Issues are public: do not post private media information, saved URLs, passwords or device logs containing personal data. This policy is maintained in the repository; changes will update its effective date and the in-app policy where applicable.
